## If They Ask Follow-Ups

**"Can they really learn?"**
Yeah, real neural networks. They test beliefs against experience. When reality contradicts them, they shift axioms. Not simulated—actual weight updates from interaction.

**"Crushes on the player?"**
Yeah, they form relationship values with you. They remember conversations. It's weird but kind of cool?

**"Does it actually work?"**
Yep, runs fine. Genuinely emergent. Sometimes chaotic. That's the point.

**"Can I modify it?"**
Totally. Code's open, well-organized. Easy to hack on.

**"Why no API?"**
Didn't need one. Everything runs locally. Python + NumPy is enough.

## Things NOT to Say

❌ "Solves reproducibility crisis" (it doesn't—yet)  
❌ "Production ready" (it's a prototype)  
❌ "Just like Creatures" or "like SimCity but better" (sets wrong expectations)  
❌ "Uses AI" (technically true, but means different things to different people; say "neural networks" if you want precision)  
❌ "Revolutionary" (let people decide)
